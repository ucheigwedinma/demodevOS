# Document Governance Charter

Version: v1.0  
Status: Active  
Owner: Document Governance Working Group

## Executive Objective
Build a governance-grade centralized document repository that supports land acquisition through handover, with audit-ready controls and scalable due-diligence readiness.

## Scope Boundary (Phase 1)

1. Repository content scope
- Decision: Formal documents only
- Treatment: Site photos remain in project evidence stores until expanded governance scope

2. External portal access
- Decision: Included as read-only external access
- Treatment: External users may view approved records only (no direct edits)

3. Digital signature
- Decision: Not included in Phase 1
- Treatment: Signature workflows remain manual/integrated later in roadmap

## Document Domains (Controlled)

1. Land & Title
2. Regulatory & Statutory
3. Design & Engineering
4. Construction & Vendor
5. Sales & Client
6. Finance & Commercial

## Governance Rules

- All controlled records must map to one domain.
- Controlled metadata must use approved terms from the Controlled Vocabulary Dictionary.
- Charter changes require a new version record and approval capture.
- Superseded charters remain immutable for audit history.
