# Controlled Vocabulary Dictionary

Version: v1.0

## Land & Title

- `deed_of_assignment`: Transfer instrument assigning legal interest in land.
- `certificate_of_occupancy`: Government-issued title certificate confirming occupancy rights.
- `survey_plan`: Licensed survey defining coordinates and parcel boundaries.

## Regulatory & Statutory

- `development_permit`: Authority approval to commence development.
- `environmental_impact_approval`: Regulatory clearance for environmental obligations.
- `compliance_certificate`: Evidence of statutory/code compliance completion.

## Design & Engineering

- `ifc_drawing`: Approved issue-for-construction drawing set.
- `as_built_drawing`: Final drawing reflecting actual built conditions.
- `design_revision`: Formal approved change to issued design.

## Construction & Vendor

- `contract_agreement`: Executed agreement defining scope, terms, and obligations.
- `variation_order`: Approved scope/value change against base contract.
- `material_approval`: Approval decision for material technical submittals.

## Sales & Client

- `offer_letter`: Commercial offer issued to buyer prospect.
- `sales_agreement`: Binding sale and purchase agreement.
- `handover_pack`: Document bundle delivered at property handover.

## Finance & Commercial

- `payment_certificate`: Certified valuation authorizing payment.
- `invoice`: Commercial invoice for goods/services supplied.
- `tax_clearance`: Official confirmation of tax compliance status.
