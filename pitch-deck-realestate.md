# developerOS for Real Estate

## The Problem

Real estate firms run on fragmented tools — spreadsheets for budgets, WhatsApp for site updates, separate systems for sales, finance, HR, and compliance. This creates:

- **Cost leakage** — budget overruns discovered too late
- **Revenue blind spots** — no real-time view of unit sales, reservations, or payment pipelines
- **Execution chaos** — project delays, missed approvals, contractor coordination failures
- **Compliance risk** — regulatory deadlines tracked manually, audit trails incomplete
- **Investor anxiety** — slow reporting, no transparency portal

---

## The Solution

**developerOS** is an integrated operations platform purpose-built for real estate development firms. One system for every department — from site to boardroom.

---

## Platform Modules

### Properties & Portfolio Management
- GPS-tagged property registry with valuations and unit breakdowns
- Ownership structures, SPV tracking, and portfolio analytics
- Unit inventory matrix — know what's available, reserved, sold, or overdue at any moment

### Project Command Center
- Multi-site project tracking with phases, milestones, and Gantt views
- Budget vs. actual monitoring with variance alerts
- Contractor task allocation and progress tracking
- Delay/risk flags and compliance approval logs

### Finance & Revenue Intelligence
- Accounts payable and receivable with automated invoicing
- General ledger, budgets, and financial reporting
- SPV-level financial management and investor tracking
- Tax management and multi-currency support

### CRM & Sales Pipeline
- Lead management with scoring and pipeline stages
- Broker management and commission tracking
- Reservation and payment schedule tracking
- Marketing campaign management
- Sales analytics and forecasting

### Procurement & Vendor Management
- Purchase requisitions, RFQs, and purchase orders
- Vendor evaluation and performance tracking
- Goods receipt and tender comparison
- Full approval workflows with audit trails

### Contracts & Compliance
- Contract creation, execution tracking, and renewal management
- Variation orders and obligation tracking
- Regulatory compliance monitoring with violation logging
- Audit scheduling and evidence collection

### HR & Workforce
- Organization structure and employee directory
- Recruitment, onboarding, and performance management
- Payroll, attendance tracking, and leave management
- Learning management and workforce analytics

### Documents & Data Drive
- Centralized document repository with version control
- Approval workflows and governance
- PDF generation, e-signatures, and OCR
- Full audit trail on every document action

### Reports & Analytics
- Cross-module dashboards and KPI visualization
- Revenue, cost, and trend analysis
- Scheduled report delivery and custom views
- Drill-through from summary to transaction detail
